The market's annual rent growth rate was below the national average of 2.4%. Occupancy decreased from 94.5% in 1Q23 to 94.1% in 2Q23. Since 4Q01, annual effective rent growth has averaged 3.1% and occupancy has averaged 93.1%.

## Market performance

- **Effective rent** increased 1.1%, from $1,144 in 1Q23 to $1,158 in 2Q23: an annual growth rate of 0.1%.
- **Ranking:** out of 268 markets ranked nationally, Clarksville was 145th for quarterly effective rent growth and 235th for annual effective rent growth in 2Q23.
- **Occupancy** fell from 94.5% to 94.1%, down from 97.7% a year earlier, and below the national average of 94.7%.
