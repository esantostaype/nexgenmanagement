Some apartment owners think that turning a profit is easy: stay on top of work orders, collect the rent, keep the community quiet, and the money will follow. Those daily tasks are crucial, but they are not enough to truly compete. A few common mistakes can quickly erode an owner's competitive edge and returns.

## 1. Not hiring the right people

Most owners say they have the best people working for them, but how do you recruit, train and retain them, and when should you hire versus outsource?

A **Certified Apartment Manager (CAM)**, an experienced property manager credentialed by the National Apartment Association, can be the answer. Like an experienced Realtor for buyers and sellers, a CAM lives and breathes property management: industry essentials, marketing, risk management, legal responsibilities, human resources, resident relations, maintenance and financial management.
