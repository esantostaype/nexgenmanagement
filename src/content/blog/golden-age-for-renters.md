High mortgage rates, record home prices and limited inventory have made buying harder than ever, while a construction wave has filled many markets, Nashville among them, with brand-new apartments. To fill units, operators are offering concessions such as weeks of free rent, waived fees and upgraded amenities.

## What it means for the market

- **More supply:** over 1.6 million new units were delivered between 2020 and 2023, with about a million more expected in 2024 and 2025.
- **Concessions are common:** roughly a third of listings on Zillow offered them, versus about 10% in 2019.
- **Class A dominates:** the newest buildings now account for more than half of the apartment market.

## The window is closing

With construction starts slowing sharply, analysts expect the best deals for renters to taper off toward late 2025 and 2026, as the supply wave is absorbed.
