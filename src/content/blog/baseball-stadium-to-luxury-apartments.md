Bush Stadium, home of the Indianapolis Indians from 1931 to 1996, sat abandoned for years and was at risk of demolition. A group of local developers acquired it for $1 through a historic-preservation nonprofit and converted it into **Stadium Lofts**: 138 apartments that were fully leased by the time they opened in 2013.

## The project in numbers

- **Budget:** about $14 million, financed with partner equity, a mortgage and city grants.
- **Units:** 95 one-bedrooms, 26 two-bedrooms and 17 lofts, with rents of roughly $900 to $1,700.
- **Phase two:** 144 more apartments in four adjacent buildings, Stadium Flats, also fully leased on opening.

## Keeping the character

The developers kept the scoreboard, ticket booths and press box, and added chain-link balconies and a courtyard by the field, so residents feel they are walking into a historic ballpark rather than an apartment building.
