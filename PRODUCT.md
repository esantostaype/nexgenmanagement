## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: property owners and investors** of multifamily assets (apartment communities, townhomes) in Tennessee, Kentucky and Alabama. They are evaluating a management company, or an acquisition, and need to trust that Nex-Gen will protect the asset, report clearly and run operations well. Their job on the site: understand what Nex-Gen does, believe it, and request a management proposal.
- **Secondary: residents and prospective residents** looking for an apartment in one of the managed communities, or current residents who need to pay rent or request maintenance.
- **Secondary: home buyers and sellers** in Clarksville, TN (Nex-Gen Home Sales).
- Tertiary: job seekers (careers page).

## Product Purpose

Nex-Gen Management, LLC is a multifamily property management company based in Clarksville, TN. It manages residential (and some commercial) communities for owners and investors, and offers residential real estate sales. Success for the website: qualified owner/investor leads (management proposal requests), with clear paths for residents and home sales visitors.

## Positioning

A hands-on, regional operator: 38 managed communities across 15 cities in TN, KY and AL, run from Clarksville by a named team, with weekly reporting and monthly financials for owners, and a property-management perspective on home sales.
